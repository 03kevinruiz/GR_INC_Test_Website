Work We're Proud Of

Every project below was completed by our team. Real scopes, real locations, real results — across Washington DC and Maryland.

Kitchen Remodel

Full Kitchen Remodel — Down to the Studs

Northwest DC

A complete kitchen taken back to its framing and rebuilt. We stripped the room to the studs, rebuilt and refinished the walls, then installed new cabinets, marble countertops, sinks, and appliances. The job included all-new electrical and plumbing along with full finish painting — a true top-to-bottom remodel built with high-end materials and fixtures throughout.

Bathroom Renovation

Three-Bathroom Renovation

Northwest DC

Three full bathrooms renovated in a single home. We stripped the dated wallpaper, repaired the walls, and repainted each space for a clean, updated finish across all three.

Whole-Home Renovation

Whole-Home Multi-Room Renovation

Northwest DC

A renovation spanning the primary bedroom, several additional rooms, and multiple bathrooms. In the primary bedroom we removed the original upholstered wall covering, replaced the carpet with new hardwood flooring, installed marble countertops, and repainted the space. Throughout the rest of the home we stripped old wallpaper, repaired the walls, and repainted, and renovated several bathrooms along the way.

Basement Conversion

Basement Apartment Conversion

Montgomery County, MD

A full basement transformed into a complete, self-contained apartment — kitchen, living room, bedroom, and full bathroom. We handled the entire conversion, from framing and flooring to the electrical and plumbing needed to turn unfinished space into a move-in-ready home.

Deck Build

Custom Deck Build

Montgomery County, MD

A custom deck built from the ground up, with a storage shed integrated underneath. We took on the full build — structure, decking, and finish — and put the space below to work with a built-in shed.
